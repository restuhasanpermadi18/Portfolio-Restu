Tokopedia Loyalty Reward

A reward that unlocks after a user's third purchase, to bring them back for a fourth.

Type
PRD for a case exercise. The retention figure comes from the case. The document reports no user testing.
Document
A PRD written so that a clickable mobile prototype can be built from it
Keywords
E-commerce, retention, personalisation, prioritisation, success metrics, PRD

The problem

In the case, only about 12% of Tokopedia buyers go on to a fourth purchase after their third. The case's explanation is that some users arrive for promotions and look at other marketplaces once the promotion ends. I treated that as a hypothesis to validate, not a fact, and the PRD says so.

The solution

After purchase 3, the user unlocks a personalised reward. In the prototype it is a discount voucher, for example Rp 30,000 off with a minimum purchase of Rp 150,000. The amounts are illustrative.

PriorityFeature
P0Personalised reward, unlocked after purchase 3, shown with its value and expiry, applied automatically at checkout
P1Homepage that shows the active reward and products it applies to
P1Push notification when the reward unlocks and before it expires

The flow runs in six screens, from "purchase 3 complete" to "purchase 4 complete".

How I'd judge it

The main metric is retention from the third to the fourth purchase: from the 12% baseline to a 20% target in six months. The second is how many eligible users redeem the reward.

I deliberately left out app opens, notification clicks and homepage visits as success measures. A fourth purchase is what counts.

Non-goals. It does not target all users at first, it does not replace Tokopedia's existing promotions, and it does not optimise engagement for its own sake.

Assumptions still to validate. That users who stop after the third order respond to rewards, that a personalised reward beats a generic promotion, and that showing relevant products next to the reward helps them find a reason to buy. The 20% target is a goal, not a forecast.
