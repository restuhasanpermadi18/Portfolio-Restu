Amazon sales dashboard in Power BI

What a sales dataset says about cancellations, seasons, categories and places.

Type
University team assignment with Jenna Gozali (Group 3), on a supplied Amazon sales dataset of 8 normalised tables
My part
I built the Power BI dashboard. Teammates handled the data model and slides.
Keywords
Retail analytics, Power BI, DAX, Power Query, data cleaning, insight writing

What the data says

Overall. The dataset shows total sales of 78.6M from 116K units and 120K orders.

Cancellations. The cancel rate is 14.28%, almost 1 in 7 orders. That is revenue lost before delivery.

Seasons. Monthly sales sit flat most of the year. From April to June they jump 6 to 7 times, then drop back to normal in July.

Categories. Set and Kurta sell about the same share of units (38.83% and 38.61%). But Set brings 49.88% of sales value and Kurta 27.1%, so Set earns nearly twice as much. Set has the higher price per unit.

Places. Bengaluru leads the cities, then Hyderabad, Mumbai, New Delhi and Chennai. The ranking is identical by value and by units. Maharashtra is about 1.3 times Karnataka, the second region.

What I recommended

  • Find the root cause of the cancel rate, probably in fulfilment or delivery.
  • Prepare stock and warehouse capacity before April to June next year.
  • Bundle or upsell Kurta, where volume is high but value per unit lags.
  • Strengthen fulfilment in the top cities and in Maharashtra and Karnataka. Test local promotions in runner-up cities.

The business is concentrated in two categories, three states and three months. That is efficient but fragile if any one of them is disrupted.

Cleaning decisions

The dataset had corrupted headers and merged cells in the category table, stray documentation rows, mixed text and date values in the order dates, and a blank row in the location table.

Many order lines had no amount, mostly from cancelled orders. I kept those rows and excluded them from the sales measure with a DAX CALCULATE filter, instead of deleting them.

What the dashboard contains

Four KPI cards (sales, units, orders, cancel rate), bar charts by city and state, a donut by category, a monthly trend, a status slicer and a toggle between sales and units, built with bookmarks and buttons.
